Historical → Natural phenomena → Psychology → Unusual inventions → Interesting comparisons →
Mind-blowing), so the same category never appears twice in a row.

**Balance:** Human body facts 17 · Animal facts 17 · Geography facts 17 · Science facts 17 · Amazing facts 16 · Historical discoveries 16 · Natural phenomena 16 · Psychology facts 16 · Unusual inventions 16 · Interesting comparisons 16 · Mind-blowing discoveries 16 (total 180).

Harappan sites, Kutch) spaced well apart, myths avoided or busted (the tongue map and the camel hump are presented as myth-busting;
no Great-Wall-type myths), contested or fast-changing numbers left out or rounded
with "about".
