Format: `N. dDDD-vV · Category · species/scene · one-line concept`

Underwater worlds 16 · Beautiful landscapes 16 · Nature cinematics 16 · Ocean life 16 · Natural environments 16.

related pairs (Underwater/Ocean, Cute/Adventures, Landscapes/Environments) are never back to back.

Mascots (fictional, only in Cute AI animals + Animal adventures): 23 seeds. Momo 10 solo, Pebble 10 solo, 3 together.
Fantasy animals: all fictional. Animal facts: 17, each with a named source.
