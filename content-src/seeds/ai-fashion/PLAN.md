## Rules this plan satisfies (checked by script)

- **Models:** 168 solo + 12 duo seeds. Appearances: Meher 48, Valeria 48, Hana 48, Elara 48
  (each 42 solo + 6 duo; every pair of models shares exactly 2 duos). The same single model never appears in two consecutive
  videos, and a duo never sits next to a solo video of one of its two models.
- **Categories:** the 13 template categories, 14 each, except Summer fashion and Fashion photography (13 each). Never the same category twice in a row.
- **Seasonality:** days 1–45 carry 8 each of Party outfits, Winter fashion and Traditional Indian outfits (vs 6 each in days 46–90),
  25 of the 45 Indian-glam looks, Diwali-week festive look (#8), winter-wedding looks, holiday parties (#88, #98–#102) and New Year's Eve (#109–#113).
  Summer fashion and Luxury destinations lean to days 46–90 (January travel and Goa season). Beach looks run throughout.
- **Style mix:** 45 Indian-glam seeds (25%): all 14 Traditional Indian outfits plus 31 in other categories
  (Party outfits 5, Beauty and styling 5, Luxury destinations 4, Trending fashion reels 4, Instagram-style fashion trends 3, Fashion photography 3, AI fashion models 3, Winter fashion 2, Luxury lifestyle 2). The other 135 are Western glam, beach, street, athleisure and winter glam.
- **Audio:** seeds where N mod 5 is 2 or 4 get a short confident Hindi/Hinglish voice-over of 8 words or fewer (72 seeds, 40%);
  the other 108 (60%) are music-only (pop-house, Afro-house, R&B-style grooves, Indian-fusion beats, lo-fi, tropical house, jazz-lounge…).
- **Platform-safe glam:** full-length, three-quarter or portrait framing; close-ups only of face, hair, jewelry or shoes; subtle movement only
  (head turn, hair flip, glance, smile, breeze) — no walking, running, dancing or spinning; template `forbiddenTerms` never used.
- **Locations:** varied Indian and international, international ones always written as "…-like"; no real hotels or brands.
